Features

Built for real conversations

Two modes for every situation. Whether you're in a quiet café or a crowded market.

Hands-Free Mode

Automatic voice detection handles turn-taking for you. Just speak naturally - Cosmo knows when to listen and when to translate. Set it down and forget about it.

Press & Hold Mode

Perfect for noisy environments and precise turn-taking. Tap and hold to speak, release to translate. Full control, no noise bleed.

50+ Languages

From Spanish and Mandarin to Japanese, Arabic, Korean and beyond. Cosmo covers the languages you actually encounter when you travel.

Live Conversation Log

Every exchange is logged in both languages in real time, so you can scroll back, reference what was said, or catch anything you missed.

Interruptions Mode

Cosmo keeps listening even while translated audio plays, so the conversation flows naturally without awkward pauses or waiting.

No Account to Start

Get 25 free guest credits on launch - no sign-up, no credit card. Translate your first conversations before you decide to create an account.
